## The short version

Notion AI and Google Workspace AI are both productivity AI tools that help you write, summarize, and organize — but they're embedded in different places. Notion AI lives inside Notion, a workspace platform where teams store documentation, manage projects, and build knowledge bases. Google Workspace AI lives inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Meet.

The decision almost always traces back to one question: where does your team's work live? That's your answer.

## What Notion AI does uniquely well

Workspace Q&A is Notion AI's standout feature. Because all your team's documentation, meeting notes, SOPs, and decisions are inside Notion, you can ask questions and get answers that reference your actual content. "What's our policy on X?" — Notion AI finds the page, reads it, and answers. "What did we decide about the product roadmap?" — Notion AI surfaces the relevant meeting notes.

This turns a knowledge base into a conversational interface. The value grows proportionally with how much your team has documented in Notion. For knowledge-heavy teams — engineering, product, operations — it can meaningfully reduce the time spent searching for information.

Notion AI also generates content in the context of your workspace: it can expand a short outline into a full document, summarize a database, or draft content in the same place where you take notes and manage projects.

## What Google Workspace AI does uniquely well

Coverage across the full suite is Google Workspace AI's structural advantage. AI features are embedded in every major Google tool: Docs for drafting and rewriting, Gmail for "Help me write" and thread summarization, Sheets for formula generation and data analysis, Meet for automated transcription and meeting summaries. You don't switch to a separate AI tool — the capability appears in each product where you're already working.

The Meet integration is particularly practical. AI meeting summaries happen automatically and are available in the Google Meet interface immediately after the call. For distributed teams running frequent video meetings, this removes significant manual note-taking effort.

Google Search integration also means that Workspace AI answers can draw on live web information — something Notion AI, working from your private workspace content, doesn't offer.

## Which should you choose

If your team uses Notion as its primary hub for documentation and knowledge: Notion AI at $8–10/month per user is worth the add-on. The Q&A capability becomes more valuable over time as your workspace grows. If your team runs on Google Workspace: Gemini features are included in Business Standard — no separate decision required. If you're choosing your primary productivity platform and AI is part of the consideration: pick the platform that fits your workflow, not the one with the best AI feature list. The AI capabilities in both are table stakes for modern productivity suites.
