### Runway

Runway is Professional AI video generation and editing platform with Gen-3 Alpha model producing the highest-quality AI videos available in 2026. With scores of 92/100 for output quality and 78/100 for ease of use, it targets Video creators who need reliable video capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $95/user/mo, positioning it as a premium option in the video space.

Key strengths include gen-3 alpha produces highest quality ai video clips and professional video editing suite with ai-powered tools. The main trade-offs are generated clips limited to 10 seconds on most plans and $15/mo standard only includes 625 credits. Runway is best suited for video creators, marketing teams, filmmakers.

### AIVA

AIVA is AI composer specializing in classical, cinematic, and soundtrack music generation with MIDI export and professional orchestration capabilities. Scoring 80/100 for quality and 75/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Film composers looking for dependable music tools. Plans start at Free – $33/mo, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include strongest at classical, cinematic, and orchestral music and midi export enables professional editing in daws. Limitations include weak at modern genres (pop, hip-hop, electronic) and no vocal generation — instrumental only. AIVA works best for film composers, game audio designers, video producers.
