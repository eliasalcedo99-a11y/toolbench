### Microsoft Designer

Microsoft Designer is Microsoft's AI design tool using DALL-E for image generation and template-based design creation, integrated with Microsoft 365 ecosystem. With scores of 68/100 for output quality and 82/100 for ease of use, it targets Microsoft users who need reliable design capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $20/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the design space.

Key strengths include free dall-e 3 image generation and integration with microsoft 365 apps. The main trade-offs are design quality and template variety far behind canva and limited customization options. Microsoft Designer is best suited for microsoft users, budget users, office workers.

### HubSpot ChatSpot

HubSpot ChatSpot is AI-powered assistant for HubSpot CRM that handles sales prospecting, contact management, report generation, and email drafting through natural language. Scoring 74/100 for quality and 82/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Sales reps looking for dependable business tools. Plans start at Free – included with HubSpot, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include free for all hubspot users including free crm and natural language queries: "show deals closing this month". Limitations include only useful with hubspot crm — no standalone value and ai capabilities are basic compared to dedicated tools. HubSpot ChatSpot works best for sales reps, marketers, smb operators.
