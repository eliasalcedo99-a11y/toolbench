### Opus Clip

Opus Clip is AI-powered video clipping tool that automatically extracts the best short clips from long-form YouTube and podcast content for social media. With scores of 78/100 for output quality and 92/100 for ease of use, it targets YouTubers who need reliable video capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $29/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the video space.

Key strengths include ai identifies the most engaging moments automatically and adds captions, reframing, and b-roll automatically. The main trade-offs are limited to repurposing — cannot create original video and ai clip selection sometimes misses the best moments. Opus Clip is best suited for youtubers, podcasters, social media managers.

### HubSpot ChatSpot

HubSpot ChatSpot is AI-powered assistant for HubSpot CRM that handles sales prospecting, contact management, report generation, and email drafting through natural language. Scoring 74/100 for quality and 82/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Sales reps looking for dependable business tools. Plans start at Free – included with HubSpot, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include free for all hubspot users including free crm and natural language queries: "show deals closing this month". Limitations include only useful with hubspot crm — no standalone value and ai capabilities are basic compared to dedicated tools. HubSpot ChatSpot works best for sales reps, marketers, smb operators.
