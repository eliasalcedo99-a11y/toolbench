### Consensus

Consensus is AI-powered academic search engine that finds and summarizes peer-reviewed research papers, providing evidence-based answers with citations. With scores of 84/100 for output quality and 88/100 for ease of use, it targets Academic researchers who need reliable search capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $12/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the search space.

Key strengths include searches 200m+ peer-reviewed papers and ai synthesizes findings across multiple studies. The main trade-offs are limited to academic papers — no web, news, or general search and ai summaries can oversimplify complex research. Consensus is best suited for academic researchers, graduate students, policy analysts.

### Canva Magic Design

Canva Magic Design is AI-powered design tool within Canva that generates complete designs from text descriptions, handles background removal, and powers Magic Write content generation. Scoring 78/100 for quality and 96/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Small business owners looking for dependable design tools. Plans start at Free – $13/mo, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include generate complete designs from text descriptions and background remover, magic eraser, and magic expand. Limitations include designs can look "canva-ish" — template-driven aesthetic and not suitable for complex ui/ux or brand design. Canva Magic Design works best for small business owners, social media managers, educators.
