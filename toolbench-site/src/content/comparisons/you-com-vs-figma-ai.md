### You.com

You.com is Privacy-focused AI search engine combining web results with AI chat, code generation, and image creation in a single search interface. With scores of 72/100 for output quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it targets Privacy-conscious users who need reliable search capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $20/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the search space.

Key strengths include free tier with ai chat, code, and image generation and privacy-focused with no ad profiling. The main trade-offs are search result quality trails google and kagi and ai features are basic compared to dedicated tools. You.com is best suited for privacy-conscious users, budget users, students.

### Figma AI

Figma AI is AI-powered design features integrated into Figma, automating layout generation, asset creation, prototyping, and design system maintenance. Scoring 85/100 for quality and 78/100 for ease of use, it appeals to UI/UX designers looking for dependable design tools. Plans start at Free – $75/editor/mo, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include ai auto-layout generates responsive designs from descriptions and built into the tool designers already use daily. Limitations include ai features require professional plan or above and output quality depends heavily on prompt specificity. Figma AI works best for ui/ux designers, product teams, design systems teams.
