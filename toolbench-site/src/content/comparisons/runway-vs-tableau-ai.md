### Runway

Runway is Professional AI video generation and editing platform with Gen-3 Alpha model producing the highest-quality AI videos available in 2026. With scores of 92/100 for output quality and 78/100 for ease of use, it targets Video creators who need reliable video capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $95/user/mo, positioning it as a premium option in the video space.

Key strengths include gen-3 alpha produces highest quality ai video clips and professional video editing suite with ai-powered tools. The main trade-offs are generated clips limited to 10 seconds on most plans and $15/mo standard only includes 625 credits. Runway is best suited for video creators, marketing teams, filmmakers.

### Tableau AI

Tableau AI is Salesforce-owned data visualization platform with AI-powered Explain Data, natural language queries, and Einstein-powered predictive analytics. Scoring 90/100 for quality and 65/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Data analysts looking for dependable business tools. Plans start at $15 – $75/user/mo, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include best-in-class data visualization and dashboard design and einstein ai provides automated explanations of data patterns. Limitations include expensive — $42/mo explorer minimum for meaningful use and steeper learning curve than power bi for beginners. Tableau AI works best for data analysts, salesforce orgs, data storytellers.
