### Synthesia

Synthesia is AI avatar video platform creating professional talking-head videos in 140+ languages without cameras, studios, or actors. With scores of 80/100 for output quality and 90/100 for ease of use, it targets L&D teams who need reliable video capabilities. Pricing starts at $22 – $67/mo, positioning it as a premium option in the video space.

Key strengths include 160+ realistic ai avatars — no camera needed and 140+ language support with lip-sync. The main trade-offs are avatars still have uncanny valley effect in close-ups and no custom avatar on starter plan. Synthesia is best suited for l&d teams, global companies, educators.

### Microsoft Designer

Microsoft Designer is Microsoft's AI design tool using DALL-E for image generation and template-based design creation, integrated with Microsoft 365 ecosystem. Scoring 68/100 for quality and 82/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Microsoft users looking for dependable design tools. Plans start at Free – $20/mo, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include free dall-e 3 image generation and integration with microsoft 365 apps. Limitations include design quality and template variety far behind canva and limited customization options. Microsoft Designer works best for microsoft users, budget users, office workers.
