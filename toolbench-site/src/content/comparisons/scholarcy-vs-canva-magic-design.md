### Scholarcy

Scholarcy is AI-powered academic paper summarizer that creates structured flashcard summaries, extracts key findings, tables, and references from research papers. With scores of 76/100 for output quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it targets PhD students who need reliable search capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $10/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the search space.

Key strengths include creates structured summary flashcards from papers and extracts key findings, methods, and conclusions. The main trade-offs are very niche — only useful for academic paper reading and summaries miss nuance on complex methodology. Scholarcy is best suited for phd students, researchers, analysts.

### Canva Magic Design

Canva Magic Design is AI-powered design tool within Canva that generates complete designs from text descriptions, handles background removal, and powers Magic Write content generation. Scoring 78/100 for quality and 96/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Small business owners looking for dependable design tools. Plans start at Free – $13/mo, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include generate complete designs from text descriptions and background remover, magic eraser, and magic expand. Limitations include designs can look "canva-ish" — template-driven aesthetic and not suitable for complex ui/ux or brand design. Canva Magic Design works best for small business owners, social media managers, educators.
