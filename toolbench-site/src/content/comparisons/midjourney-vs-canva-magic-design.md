### Midjourney

Midjourney is The highest-quality AI image generator, producing photorealistic and artistic images through Discord-based and web-based interfaces. With scores of 98/100 for output quality and 70/100 for ease of use, it targets Graphic designers who need reliable image capabilities. Pricing starts at $10 – $120/mo, positioning it as a premium option in the image space.

Key strengths include highest image quality and aesthetic consistency among all generators and v6 model produces near-photorealistic images with excellent composition. The main trade-offs are no free tier — $10/mo minimum to start and discord-based workflow alienates non-technical users. Midjourney is best suited for graphic designers, marketing creatives, game/concept artists.

### Canva Magic Design

Canva Magic Design is AI-powered design tool within Canva that generates complete designs from text descriptions, handles background removal, and powers Magic Write content generation. Scoring 78/100 for quality and 96/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Small business owners looking for dependable design tools. Plans start at Free – $13/mo, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include generate complete designs from text descriptions and background remover, magic eraser, and magic expand. Limitations include designs can look "canva-ish" — template-driven aesthetic and not suitable for complex ui/ux or brand design. Canva Magic Design works best for small business owners, social media managers, educators.
