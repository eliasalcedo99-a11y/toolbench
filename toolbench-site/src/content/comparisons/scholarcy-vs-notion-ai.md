### Scholarcy

Scholarcy is AI-powered academic paper summarizer that creates structured flashcard summaries, extracts key findings, tables, and references from research papers. With scores of 76/100 for output quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it targets PhD students who need reliable search capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $10/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the search space.

Key strengths include creates structured summary flashcards from papers and extracts key findings, methods, and conclusions. The main trade-offs are very niche — only useful for academic paper reading and summaries miss nuance on complex methodology. Scholarcy is best suited for phd students, researchers, analysts.

### Notion AI

Notion AI is AI assistant integrated into Notion's workspace platform, offering writing assistance, summarization, database automation, and Q&A across your workspace. Scoring 82/100 for quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Knowledge workers looking for dependable productivity tools. Plans start at Free – $10/member/mo + $10/mo AI, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include ai works across all your notion pages and databases and q&a answers questions from your entire workspace. Limitations include $10/mo ai add-on is on top of notion subscription and ai quality is good but not best-in-class for any single task. Notion AI works best for knowledge workers, team leads, project managers.
