## The short version

Runway and Synthesia are both called AI video tools, which makes them sound like direct competitors. They're not. They solve different problems, and understanding what each does is more useful than comparing them head-to-head.

Runway is a generative video tool: it creates video footage from text prompts, images, or existing clips using Gen-3 Alpha, its AI model. You use it to produce visual content — short clips, motion effects, AI video generation. Think of it as an AI video camera and effects suite.

Synthesia is an avatar video tool: it generates talking-head videos where an AI presenter reads your script. You use it to produce presenter-led content — training videos, product explainers, tutorials. Think of it as a virtual studio with a digital host.

## When to use Runway

Runway is the choice for visual creators who need to produce video footage with AI. Its Gen-3 Alpha model can generate cinematic clips from text descriptions — a coastal sunset, a futuristic cityscape, a product showcase in motion — that serve as visual b-roll, conceptual visualization, or standalone creative content.

Beyond generation, Runway has a full AI video editing suite: motion brush (adding movement to still images), Inpainting (removing or replacing parts of videos), and various stylization tools. For filmmakers, visual directors, and creative teams who produce motion content, these tools add capabilities that traditional editing software can't match.

The API is also more accessible than Synthesia's, making it usable for developers building video generation into products.

## When to use Synthesia

Synthesia is the choice for teams who need to produce presenter-led video at scale without a camera. The core value proposition is simple: you write a script, choose an AI avatar, and get a professional-looking video of that presenter reading it in any of 140+ languages. No studio booking, no actor scheduling, no reshooting when the script changes — just update the text and regenerate.

For corporate L&D teams, this changes the economics of training video production. A 20-minute training course that might require a studio day can be produced and updated in hours. LMS integration means the content flows directly into your training platform.

## Which should you buy

Buy Runway if you're a creative, filmmaker, or marketing team producing visual content who wants generative AI video capability. Buy Synthesia if you're an L&D team, educator, or corporate communicator who needs to produce presenter-led video at scale without filming. If you need both types — generative footage for visual campaigns and avatar video for presenter-led explainers — they're complementary tools that serve genuinely different production needs.
