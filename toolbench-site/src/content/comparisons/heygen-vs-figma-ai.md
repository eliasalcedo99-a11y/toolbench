### HeyGen

HeyGen is AI video platform specializing in talking avatar videos with best-in-class lip-sync, voice cloning, and video localization across languages. With scores of 82/100 for output quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it targets Global marketers who need reliable video capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $89/mo, positioning it as a premium option in the video space.

Key strengths include industry-leading lip-sync quality and voice cloning creates natural-sounding ai voices. The main trade-offs are free tier very limited (1 min/month) and avatar quality slightly below synthesia. HeyGen is best suited for global marketers, course creators, content creators.

### Figma AI

Figma AI is AI-powered design features integrated into Figma, automating layout generation, asset creation, prototyping, and design system maintenance. Scoring 85/100 for quality and 78/100 for ease of use, it appeals to UI/UX designers looking for dependable design tools. Plans start at Free – $75/editor/mo, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include ai auto-layout generates responsive designs from descriptions and built into the tool designers already use daily. Limitations include ai features require professional plan or above and output quality depends heavily on prompt specificity. Figma AI works best for ui/ux designers, product teams, design systems teams.
