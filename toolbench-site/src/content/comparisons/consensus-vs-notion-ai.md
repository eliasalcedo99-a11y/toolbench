### Consensus

Consensus is AI-powered academic search engine that finds and summarizes peer-reviewed research papers, providing evidence-based answers with citations. With scores of 84/100 for output quality and 88/100 for ease of use, it targets Academic researchers who need reliable search capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $12/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the search space.

Key strengths include searches 200m+ peer-reviewed papers and ai synthesizes findings across multiple studies. The main trade-offs are limited to academic papers — no web, news, or general search and ai summaries can oversimplify complex research. Consensus is best suited for academic researchers, graduate students, policy analysts.

### Notion AI

Notion AI is AI assistant integrated into Notion's workspace platform, offering writing assistance, summarization, database automation, and Q&A across your workspace. Scoring 82/100 for quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Knowledge workers looking for dependable productivity tools. Plans start at Free – $10/member/mo + $10/mo AI, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include ai works across all your notion pages and databases and q&a answers questions from your entire workspace. Limitations include $10/mo ai add-on is on top of notion subscription and ai quality is good but not best-in-class for any single task. Notion AI works best for knowledge workers, team leads, project managers.
