### Microsoft Designer

Microsoft Designer is Microsoft's AI design tool using DALL-E for image generation and template-based design creation, integrated with Microsoft 365 ecosystem. With scores of 68/100 for output quality and 82/100 for ease of use, it targets Microsoft users who need reliable design capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $20/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the design space.

Key strengths include free dall-e 3 image generation and integration with microsoft 365 apps. The main trade-offs are design quality and template variety far behind canva and limited customization options. Microsoft Designer is best suited for microsoft users, budget users, office workers.

### Slack AI

Slack AI is AI features built into Slack for channel summarization, thread catch-up, search enhancement, and workflow automation in team communication. Scoring 76/100 for quality and 88/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Team communicators looking for dependable productivity tools. Plans start at $9 – $13/user/mo, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include channel and thread summarization catches you up instantly and ai-enhanced search finds answers across all conversations. Limitations include only useful within slack — no external capabilities and ai summarization can miss nuance and context. Slack AI works best for team communicators, information seekers, process automators.
