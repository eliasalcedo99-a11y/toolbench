### Runway

Runway is Professional AI video generation and editing platform with Gen-3 Alpha model producing the highest-quality AI videos available in 2026. With scores of 92/100 for output quality and 78/100 for ease of use, it targets Video creators who need reliable video capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $95/user/mo, positioning it as a premium option in the video space.

Key strengths include gen-3 alpha produces highest quality ai video clips and professional video editing suite with ai-powered tools. The main trade-offs are generated clips limited to 10 seconds on most plans and $15/mo standard only includes 625 credits. Runway is best suited for video creators, marketing teams, filmmakers.

### Notion AI

Notion AI is AI assistant integrated into Notion's workspace platform, offering writing assistance, summarization, database automation, and Q&A across your workspace. Scoring 82/100 for quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Knowledge workers looking for dependable productivity tools. Plans start at Free – $10/member/mo + $10/mo AI, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include ai works across all your notion pages and databases and q&a answers questions from your entire workspace. Limitations include $10/mo ai add-on is on top of notion subscription and ai quality is good but not best-in-class for any single task. Notion AI works best for knowledge workers, team leads, project managers.
