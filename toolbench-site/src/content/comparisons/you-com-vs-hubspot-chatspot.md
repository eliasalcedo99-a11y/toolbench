### You.com

You.com is Privacy-focused AI search engine combining web results with AI chat, code generation, and image creation in a single search interface. With scores of 72/100 for output quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it targets Privacy-conscious users who need reliable search capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $20/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the search space.

Key strengths include free tier with ai chat, code, and image generation and privacy-focused with no ad profiling. The main trade-offs are search result quality trails google and kagi and ai features are basic compared to dedicated tools. You.com is best suited for privacy-conscious users, budget users, students.

### HubSpot ChatSpot

HubSpot ChatSpot is AI-powered assistant for HubSpot CRM that handles sales prospecting, contact management, report generation, and email drafting through natural language. Scoring 74/100 for quality and 82/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Sales reps looking for dependable business tools. Plans start at Free – included with HubSpot, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include free for all hubspot users including free crm and natural language queries: "show deals closing this month". Limitations include only useful with hubspot crm — no standalone value and ai capabilities are basic compared to dedicated tools. HubSpot ChatSpot works best for sales reps, marketers, smb operators.
