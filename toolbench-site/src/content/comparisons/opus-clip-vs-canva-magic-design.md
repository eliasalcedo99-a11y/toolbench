### Opus Clip

Opus Clip is AI-powered video clipping tool that automatically extracts the best short clips from long-form YouTube and podcast content for social media. With scores of 78/100 for output quality and 92/100 for ease of use, it targets YouTubers who need reliable video capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $29/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the video space.

Key strengths include ai identifies the most engaging moments automatically and adds captions, reframing, and b-roll automatically. The main trade-offs are limited to repurposing — cannot create original video and ai clip selection sometimes misses the best moments. Opus Clip is best suited for youtubers, podcasters, social media managers.

### Canva Magic Design

Canva Magic Design is AI-powered design tool within Canva that generates complete designs from text descriptions, handles background removal, and powers Magic Write content generation. Scoring 78/100 for quality and 96/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Small business owners looking for dependable design tools. Plans start at Free – $13/mo, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include generate complete designs from text descriptions and background remover, magic eraser, and magic expand. Limitations include designs can look "canva-ish" — template-driven aesthetic and not suitable for complex ui/ux or brand design. Canva Magic Design works best for small business owners, social media managers, educators.
