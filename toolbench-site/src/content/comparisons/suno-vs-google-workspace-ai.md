### Suno

Suno is The most popular AI music generator creating full songs with vocals, instrumentals, and lyrics from text prompts in virtually any genre. With scores of 82/100 for output quality and 95/100 for ease of use, it targets Content creators who need reliable music capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $30/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the music space.

Key strengths include full songs with vocals, instrumentals, and lyrics in minutes and covers virtually every music genre with surprising quality. The main trade-offs are copyright and licensing for commercial use remains murky and vocal quality can sound artificial on closer listening. Suno is best suited for content creators, game developers, app developers.

### Google Workspace AI

Google Workspace AI is Google's Duet AI (now Gemini for Workspace) bringing AI assistance to Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Meet for content creation and productivity. Scoring 80/100 for quality and 88/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Google Workspace teams looking for dependable productivity tools. Plans start at $7 – $25/user/mo, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include ai natively integrated across gmail, docs, sheets, slides, meet and help me write in gmail and docs is genuinely useful daily. Limitations include ai features require business standard ($14/user/mo) or above and quality of ai output trails standalone tools like chatgpt. Google Workspace AI works best for google workspace teams, spreadsheet users, remote teams.
