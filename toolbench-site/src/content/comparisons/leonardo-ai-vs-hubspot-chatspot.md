### Leonardo.ai

Leonardo.ai is AI image generator optimized for gaming, fantasy, and concept art with real-time canvas editing and community-trained fine-tuned models. With scores of 80/100 for output quality and 82/100 for ease of use, it targets Game artists who need reliable image capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $60/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the image space.

Key strengths include free tier offers 150 images/day — most generous free option and real-time canvas for interactive image editing. The main trade-offs are general-purpose quality trails midjourney and less effective for photorealistic content. Leonardo.ai is best suited for game artists, fantasy/sci-fi creators, concept artists.

### HubSpot ChatSpot

HubSpot ChatSpot is AI-powered assistant for HubSpot CRM that handles sales prospecting, contact management, report generation, and email drafting through natural language. Scoring 74/100 for quality and 82/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Sales reps looking for dependable business tools. Plans start at Free – included with HubSpot, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include free for all hubspot users including free crm and natural language queries: "show deals closing this month". Limitations include only useful with hubspot crm — no standalone value and ai capabilities are basic compared to dedicated tools. HubSpot ChatSpot works best for sales reps, marketers, smb operators.
