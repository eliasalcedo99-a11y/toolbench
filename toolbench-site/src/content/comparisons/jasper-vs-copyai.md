### Jasper AI

Jasper's bet is brand voice. Once you've trained a voice, the output stays in lane across long content series — meaningful if you're a 5-person team writing 8 blog posts a month and you can't have one piece sounding like a different agency wrote it.

The Teams plan at $99/mo unlocks unlimited words and three voices. For an agency or in-house team running multiple brands, that math wins fast. The friction is onboarding: budget two to three hours of structured input per voice or you'll get generic output and assume the tool is broken.

### Copy.ai

Copy.ai's bet is workflow automation. Their Workflows feature is the genuine differentiator — you can chain prompts across data sources to generate, say, 50 LinkedIn posts from a single CSV of customer interviews. Jasper has no equivalent.

The free tier (2k words/mo) is the entry point most teams actually use to evaluate, and the $36/mo Pro plan covers a solo operator's needs without compromise. Where it loses is brand voice — the single-voice limit means anything beyond a 3-piece series starts to drift.
