### Opus Clip

Opus Clip is AI-powered video clipping tool that automatically extracts the best short clips from long-form YouTube and podcast content for social media. With scores of 78/100 for output quality and 92/100 for ease of use, it targets YouTubers who need reliable video capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $29/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the video space.

Key strengths include ai identifies the most engaging moments automatically and adds captions, reframing, and b-roll automatically. The main trade-offs are limited to repurposing — cannot create original video and ai clip selection sometimes misses the best moments. Opus Clip is best suited for youtubers, podcasters, social media managers.

### Notion AI

Notion AI is AI assistant integrated into Notion's workspace platform, offering writing assistance, summarization, database automation, and Q&A across your workspace. Scoring 82/100 for quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Knowledge workers looking for dependable productivity tools. Plans start at Free – $10/member/mo + $10/mo AI, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include ai works across all your notion pages and databases and q&a answers questions from your entire workspace. Limitations include $10/mo ai add-on is on top of notion subscription and ai quality is good but not best-in-class for any single task. Notion AI works best for knowledge workers, team leads, project managers.
