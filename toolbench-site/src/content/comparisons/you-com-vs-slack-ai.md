### You.com

You.com is Privacy-focused AI search engine combining web results with AI chat, code generation, and image creation in a single search interface. With scores of 72/100 for output quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it targets Privacy-conscious users who need reliable search capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $20/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the search space.

Key strengths include free tier with ai chat, code, and image generation and privacy-focused with no ad profiling. The main trade-offs are search result quality trails google and kagi and ai features are basic compared to dedicated tools. You.com is best suited for privacy-conscious users, budget users, students.

### Slack AI

Slack AI is AI features built into Slack for channel summarization, thread catch-up, search enhancement, and workflow automation in team communication. Scoring 76/100 for quality and 88/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Team communicators looking for dependable productivity tools. Plans start at $9 – $13/user/mo, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include channel and thread summarization catches you up instantly and ai-enhanced search finds answers across all conversations. Limitations include only useful within slack — no external capabilities and ai summarization can miss nuance and context. Slack AI works best for team communicators, information seekers, process automators.
