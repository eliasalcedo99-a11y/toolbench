### Leonardo.ai

Leonardo.ai is AI image generator optimized for gaming, fantasy, and concept art with real-time canvas editing and community-trained fine-tuned models. With scores of 80/100 for output quality and 82/100 for ease of use, it targets Game artists who need reliable image capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $60/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the image space.

Key strengths include free tier offers 150 images/day — most generous free option and real-time canvas for interactive image editing. The main trade-offs are general-purpose quality trails midjourney and less effective for photorealistic content. Leonardo.ai is best suited for game artists, fantasy/sci-fi creators, concept artists.

### Canva Magic Design

Canva Magic Design is AI-powered design tool within Canva that generates complete designs from text descriptions, handles background removal, and powers Magic Write content generation. Scoring 78/100 for quality and 96/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Small business owners looking for dependable design tools. Plans start at Free – $13/mo, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include generate complete designs from text descriptions and background remover, magic eraser, and magic expand. Limitations include designs can look "canva-ish" — template-driven aesthetic and not suitable for complex ui/ux or brand design. Canva Magic Design works best for small business owners, social media managers, educators.
