### Suno

Suno is The most popular AI music generator creating full songs with vocals, instrumentals, and lyrics from text prompts in virtually any genre. With scores of 82/100 for output quality and 95/100 for ease of use, it targets Content creators who need reliable music capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $30/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the music space.

Key strengths include full songs with vocals, instrumentals, and lyrics in minutes and covers virtually every music genre with surprising quality. The main trade-offs are copyright and licensing for commercial use remains murky and vocal quality can sound artificial on closer listening. Suno is best suited for content creators, game developers, app developers.

### Canva Magic Design

Canva Magic Design is AI-powered design tool within Canva that generates complete designs from text descriptions, handles background removal, and powers Magic Write content generation. Scoring 78/100 for quality and 96/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Small business owners looking for dependable design tools. Plans start at Free – $13/mo, making it a budget-friendly choice.

Notable advantages include generate complete designs from text descriptions and background remover, magic eraser, and magic expand. Limitations include designs can look "canva-ish" — template-driven aesthetic and not suitable for complex ui/ux or brand design. Canva Magic Design works best for small business owners, social media managers, educators.
