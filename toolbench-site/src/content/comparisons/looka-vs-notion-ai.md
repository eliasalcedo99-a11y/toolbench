### Looka

Looka is AI-powered logo and brand identity generator that creates professional logos, brand kits, and marketing materials from text descriptions of your business. With scores of 72/100 for output quality and 92/100 for ease of use, it targets Startup founders who need reliable design capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $96/yr, positioning it as a competitive option in the design space.

Key strengths include generates hundreds of logo options in seconds and full brand kit with colors, fonts, and guidelines. The main trade-offs are logo quality trails professional designers and limited customization depth. Looka is best suited for startup founders, small business owners, side project creators.

### Notion AI

Notion AI is AI assistant integrated into Notion's workspace platform, offering writing assistance, summarization, database automation, and Q&A across your workspace. Scoring 82/100 for quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Knowledge workers looking for dependable productivity tools. Plans start at Free – $10/member/mo + $10/mo AI, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include ai works across all your notion pages and databases and q&a answers questions from your entire workspace. Limitations include $10/mo ai add-on is on top of notion subscription and ai quality is good but not best-in-class for any single task. Notion AI works best for knowledge workers, team leads, project managers.
