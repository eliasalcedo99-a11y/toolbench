### AIVA

AIVA is AI composer specializing in classical, cinematic, and soundtrack music generation with MIDI export and professional orchestration capabilities. With scores of 80/100 for output quality and 75/100 for ease of use, it targets Film composers who need reliable music capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $33/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the music space.

Key strengths include strongest at classical, cinematic, and orchestral music and midi export enables professional editing in daws. The main trade-offs are weak at modern genres (pop, hip-hop, electronic) and no vocal generation — instrumental only. AIVA is best suited for film composers, game audio designers, video producers.

### Tableau AI

Tableau AI is Salesforce-owned data visualization platform with AI-powered Explain Data, natural language queries, and Einstein-powered predictive analytics. Scoring 90/100 for quality and 65/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Data analysts looking for dependable business tools. Plans start at $15 – $75/user/mo, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include best-in-class data visualization and dashboard design and einstein ai provides automated explanations of data patterns. Limitations include expensive — $42/mo explorer minimum for meaningful use and steeper learning curve than power bi for beginners. Tableau AI works best for data analysts, salesforce orgs, data storytellers.
