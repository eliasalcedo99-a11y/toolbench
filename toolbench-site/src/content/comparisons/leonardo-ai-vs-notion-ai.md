### Leonardo.ai

Leonardo.ai is AI image generator optimized for gaming, fantasy, and concept art with real-time canvas editing and community-trained fine-tuned models. With scores of 80/100 for output quality and 82/100 for ease of use, it targets Game artists who need reliable image capabilities. Pricing starts at Free – $60/mo, positioning it as a competitive option in the image space.

Key strengths include free tier offers 150 images/day — most generous free option and real-time canvas for interactive image editing. The main trade-offs are general-purpose quality trails midjourney and less effective for photorealistic content. Leonardo.ai is best suited for game artists, fantasy/sci-fi creators, concept artists.

### Notion AI

Notion AI is AI assistant integrated into Notion's workspace platform, offering writing assistance, summarization, database automation, and Q&A across your workspace. Scoring 82/100 for quality and 85/100 for ease of use, it appeals to Knowledge workers looking for dependable productivity tools. Plans start at Free – $10/member/mo + $10/mo AI, making it a premium choice.

Notable advantages include ai works across all your notion pages and databases and q&a answers questions from your entire workspace. Limitations include $10/mo ai add-on is on top of notion subscription and ai quality is good but not best-in-class for any single task. Notion AI works best for knowledge workers, team leads, project managers.
