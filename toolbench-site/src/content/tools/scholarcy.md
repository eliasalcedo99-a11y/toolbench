## Overview

Scholarcy is a niche AI tool for academic paper summarization. It creates structured flashcard summaries extracting key findings, methods, and conclusions from research papers.

## Use Cases

PhD students reading dozens of papers weekly, researchers building literature reviews, and analysts extracting data from reports. r/GradSchool recommends it alongside Consensus.

## The Bottom Line

Invaluable if you read academic papers regularly. Free extension for basic use, $10/mo for full library features. Very niche — most users should look at Consensus for academic search first.
