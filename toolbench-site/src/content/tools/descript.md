## Overview

Descript revolutionized audio/video editing by letting you edit media by editing text. Acquired by Spotify in 2024, it's become the go-to tool for podcast and video creators who want a simpler editing workflow.

## Use Cases

Podcasters edit episodes by deleting words from transcripts. YouTubers use it for quick video editing. Screen recording + editing makes it popular for tutorials. r/podcasting's most recommended editing tool.

## The Bottom Line

Best for podcast and tutorial video editing. Edit-by-transcript is genuinely revolutionary. $24/mo Hobbyist for individual creators. Not a Premiere Pro replacement for complex productions.
