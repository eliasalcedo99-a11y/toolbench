## Overview

Grammarly is the most ubiquitous AI writing tool with 500M+ users. Originally a grammar checker, it evolved into a comprehensive writing assistant with generative AI (GrammarlyGO) in 2023. By 2026, it's installed on more devices than any other AI tool.

## Use Cases

Used across every profession for email, document, and message polishing. Reddit's r/writing recommends it universally. Business teams use the brand voice feature for consistent communication. Students use it for academic writing improvement.

## The Bottom Line

The one AI tool everyone should install. Free tier is genuinely useful. Premium at $12/mo for power users. Not a replacement for Jasper-style content generation — it's the quality layer that sits on top.
