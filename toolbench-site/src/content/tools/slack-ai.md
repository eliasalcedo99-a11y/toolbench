## Overview

Slack AI adds channel summarization, enhanced search, and workflow automation to the popular team communication platform. Acquired by Salesforce, it's positioning AI as a core feature rather than an add-on.

## Use Cases

Teams use channel summaries to catch up after time off, search to find past decisions, and Workflow Builder for automations. r/Slack discusses AI features. Most useful for high-volume Slack workspaces.

## The Bottom Line

Useful AI add-on for Slack-heavy teams. Included in Pro at $9/user/mo. Channel summaries are the killer feature. Not a reason to adopt Slack — but valuable if you're already there.
