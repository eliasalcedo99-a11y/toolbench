## Overview

Google Workspace AI (formerly Duet AI) brings Gemini-powered assistance across Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Meet. For the 3B+ Gmail users, it's the most accessible path to AI-enhanced productivity.

## Use Cases

Teams use Help Me Write for emails and docs, Help Me Organize for spreadsheets, and auto-summaries for meetings. r/gsuite discusses AI features regularly. Direct competitor to Microsoft 365 Copilot.

## The Bottom Line

Essential for Google Workspace organizations. $14/user/mo Business Standard unlocks AI. Direct competitor to M365 Copilot at $30/user/mo — Google wins on price, Microsoft on depth.
