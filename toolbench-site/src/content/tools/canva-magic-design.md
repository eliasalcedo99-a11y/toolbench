## Overview

Canva Magic Design brings AI to the world's most popular design platform (150M+ users). It generates complete designs from text prompts, removes backgrounds, and powers content generation — all within Canva's intuitive interface.

## Use Cases

Small businesses create marketing materials, social media managers produce daily content, educators make presentations. r/smallbusiness and r/socialmedia recommend it universally for non-designers.

## The Bottom Line

Best design AI for non-designers. Canva Pro at $13/mo is extraordinary value. For professional design work, use Figma AI. For AI image generation, Midjourney produces higher quality.
