## Overview

Otter.ai is the leading AI meeting assistant, providing real-time transcription and automated notes for meetings. OtterPilot can auto-join scheduled meetings, record, transcribe, and generate summaries with action items.

## Use Cases

Business professionals use it for meeting notes, journalists for interview transcription, students for lecture capture. r/productivity consistently recommends it. Integrations with Zoom, Teams, and Meet make it plug-and-play.

## The Bottom Line

Best AI meeting transcription tool. Free tier (600 min/mo) is generous. $17/mo Pro for power users. Essential for meeting-heavy professionals. For audio/video editing, use Descript instead.
