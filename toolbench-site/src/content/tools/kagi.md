## Overview

Kagi is the premium search engine that charges users instead of advertisers, eliminating the ad-driven incentives that degrade Google's results. By 2026, it's the default search for privacy-conscious power users and developers.

## Use Cases

Developers use it for unbiased technical search. Privacy advocates avoid Google's tracking. r/degoogle consistently recommends Kagi as the top alternative. AI summaries rival Perplexity for quick answers.

## The Bottom Line

Best search engine if you're willing to pay. $10/mo Professional is the sweet spot. Not for casual users who are fine with Google. For deep research, pair with Perplexity.
