## Overview

AIVA (Artificial Intelligence Virtual Artist) is the most established AI music generator, launched in 2016. It specializes in classical, cinematic, and orchestral music with professional MIDI export capabilities.

## Use Cases

Film composers use it for scoring assistance, game designers for adaptive soundtracks, and video producers for royalty-free cinematic music. Less mainstream than Suno but preferred by professionals working in classical genres.

## The Bottom Line

Best for classical and cinematic music. MIDI export enables DAW editing. $15/mo Standard for commercial use. For modern genres with vocals, use Suno or Udio instead.
