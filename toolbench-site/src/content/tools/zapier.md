Every SMB stack we audited in Q1 2026 had Zapier in it. That's not an accident — at 7,000+ integrations, it's the only automation platform with the breadth to be the default connective layer.

The 2026 release of Zapier Agents shifts the value prop from "trigger-action automation" to "describe an outcome and let the agent figure out the steps." For most operations work, this collapses what used to be a 7-step Zap into a single prompt.

Where Zapier loses is at high task volume. The per-task pricing model gets expensive past 5,000 tasks/month, and Make becomes the better choice. For under that threshold, Zapier's UX advantage is decisive.
