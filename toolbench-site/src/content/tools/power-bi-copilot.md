## Overview

Power BI with Copilot combines Microsoft's market-leading BI platform with AI-powered natural language queries and automated insights. Business users ask questions in plain English and get visualizations, summaries, and recommendations.

## Use Cases

Data analysts build dashboards, managers query data without SQL, and BI teams standardize org-wide reporting. r/PowerBI discusses Copilot features extensively. Enterprise adoption is strong among M365 organizations.

## The Bottom Line

Essential for Microsoft-stack organizations doing business intelligence. $10/mo Pro for analysts, Premium for Copilot features. Not for small businesses — Tableau is simpler for basic analytics.
