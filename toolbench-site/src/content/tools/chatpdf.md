## Overview

ChatPDF is a single-purpose AI tool that lets you have conversations with PDF documents. Upload a PDF and ask questions — it responds with answers and page references. Simplicity is the product.

## Use Cases

Students analyze research papers, lawyers review contracts, researchers extract data from papers. r/ChatGPT users sometimes recommend it for PDF-specific work over ChatGPT itself due to the focused UX.

## The Bottom Line

Best dedicated PDF analysis tool. Free tier for light use, $20/mo for power users. However, ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo) handles PDFs plus everything else — ChatPDF only wins on simplicity.
