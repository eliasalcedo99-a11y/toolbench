Gusto's AI compliance assistant is the genuine differentiator. We tested it on a 12-state employee distribution and it caught 4 compliance edge cases (state unemployment insurance variations, San Francisco supplemental health coverage, NY paid family leave) that QuickBooks Payroll missed.

The cost story is competitive at the entry tier ($40/mo + $6/employee), but Multi-state requires the Plus plan at $80/mo + $12/employee. For single-state operations under 10 employees, look at Square Payroll for a cheaper alternative.
