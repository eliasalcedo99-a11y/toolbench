## Overview

Leonardo.ai launched in 2023 as an AI image generator focused on gaming and fantasy art. By 2026, it serves over 10M users with the most generous free tier in the market (150 images/day). The Real-time Canvas feature allows interactive image editing.

## Use Cases

Game developers, fantasy artists, and concept designers are the core users. Reddit's r/Leonardo_AI praises the gaming-optimized models. Product Hunt reviews highlight the free tier generosity. Used for character design, environment art, and concept visualization.

## The Bottom Line

Best free option for image generation and strongest for gaming/fantasy art. $12/mo Apprentice for professionals. For general photorealism, Midjourney is superior. For API needs, DALL-E 3 or Stable Diffusion are better choices.
