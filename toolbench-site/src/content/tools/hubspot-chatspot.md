## Overview

HubSpot ChatSpot is an AI assistant built into HubSpot's CRM, allowing users to interact with their sales and marketing data through natural language. Free for all HubSpot users, it adds AI capabilities to the leading SMB CRM platform.

## Use Cases

Sales reps query pipeline data, marketers generate reports, and managers draft CRM-informed emails. r/sales and r/marketing discuss HubSpot's AI features. Most useful for teams already invested in HubSpot.

## The Bottom Line

Free and useful if you're a HubSpot user. Not a reason to switch CRMs. For standalone AI assistants, ChatGPT or Claude are far more capable.
