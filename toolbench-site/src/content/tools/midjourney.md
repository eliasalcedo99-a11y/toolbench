## Overview

Midjourney is the gold standard for AI image generation in 2026. Created by David Holz's independent research lab, it consistently produces the most aesthetically pleasing images across all AI generators. The V6 model launched in late 2024 brought near-photorealistic quality.

Designers, marketers, and artists use Midjourney for concept art, campaign visuals, product mockups, and social media content. Reddit's r/midjourney (2M+ members) is one of the most active AI communities. Product Hunt rates it as the #1 AI image tool.

## The Bottom Line

If image quality is your priority, Midjourney has no equal. $10/mo Basic covers casual needs. The Discord workflow is the main friction point — the web app is catching up. For programmatic image generation, use DALL-E 3's API instead.
