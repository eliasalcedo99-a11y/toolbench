## Overview

Adobe Express is Adobe's answer to Canva, combining template-based design with Firefly AI for commercially safe image generation. At $10/mo Premium, it undercuts Canva Pro ($13/mo) while offering Adobe ecosystem integration.

## Use Cases

Adobe CC users create quick social graphics and marketing materials. Teams needing IP-safe AI images. Marketers creating branded content. Less popular than Canva but preferred by Adobe loyalists.

## The Bottom Line

Best Canva alternative for Adobe users. $10/mo Premium is competitive. Commercial safety via Firefly is the key differentiator. Canva still has better templates and ease of use.
