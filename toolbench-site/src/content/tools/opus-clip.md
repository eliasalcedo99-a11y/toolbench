## Overview

Opus Clip automates the tedious process of extracting short clips from long-form video. Feed it a YouTube URL or upload a video, and it identifies the most engaging moments, adds captions, reframes for vertical, and even predicts virality.

## Use Cases

YouTubers repurpose long videos into Shorts. Podcasters extract highlight clips. Social media managers create vertical content. r/NewTubers frequently recommends it. Product Hunt reviews praise the time savings.

## The Bottom Line

Essential for anyone repurposing long-form video. $9/mo Starter covers most creators. Not for original video creation — use Runway for that. Best for YouTubers, podcasters, and social media teams.
