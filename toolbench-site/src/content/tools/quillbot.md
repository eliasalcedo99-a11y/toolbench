## Overview

Quillbot is the most popular AI paraphrasing tool, widely used by students and ESL writers. Acquired by Course Hero (now part of the Learneo family), it serves millions of users who need to rewrite and improve text quickly and affordably.

## Use Cases

Dominant in academic settings for paraphrasing research and improving writing clarity. Reddit's r/college frequently recommends it alongside Grammarly. ESL writers use it to improve fluency in English writing.

## The Bottom Line

Best paraphrasing tool at a budget price. Premium at $10/mo is half Grammarly's cost. Not a content creator — use it to rewrite and improve existing text. For generating new content, look at Jasper or Copy.ai.
