## Overview

You.com positions itself as the privacy-focused AI search engine with built-in chat, code, and image generation. Free to use with no ad profiling, it appeals to users who want AI search without the privacy trade-offs of Google.

## Use Cases

Used by privacy-conscious users as a Google alternative. Students use it for free AI-powered research. Less popular than Perplexity but fully free without restrictions.

## The Bottom Line

Solid free AI search alternative. Quality trails Perplexity and Kagi. Best for users who want basic AI search at zero cost.
