## Overview

Consensus is an AI-powered search engine for peer-reviewed research, searching 200M+ academic papers and synthesizing findings with citations. The Consensus Meter visualizes scientific agreement on any topic.

## Use Cases

Researchers conduct literature reviews, students find evidence for papers, and analysts source data for reports. r/academia recommends it alongside Google Scholar. Product Hunt praised it for democratizing research access.

## The Bottom Line

Best academic search tool. Free for basic searches, $12/mo Premium for AI synthesis. Essential for anyone who needs peer-reviewed evidence. Pair with Perplexity for general research.
