## Overview

Copy.ai positions itself as the workflow-first AI writing tool. Its Workflows feature chains prompts across data sources to automate content production — a differentiator Jasper lacks. The free tier with 2K words/month makes it the easiest AI writing tool to evaluate.

## Use Cases

Solo operators and social media managers use Copy.ai for short-form: LinkedIn posts, ad copy, email subject lines, and product descriptions. The Workflows feature is popular for batch content generation.

## The Bottom Line

Best entry point for AI writing. Free tier for evaluation, $36/mo Pro for daily use. Upgrade to Jasper when brand voice consistency across a team becomes critical.
