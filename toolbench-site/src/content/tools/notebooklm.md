## Overview

Google NotebookLM went viral in late 2024 for its Audio Overview feature — upload documents and get a surprisingly natural AI-generated podcast discussing them. By 2026 it remains completely free and is used by millions of students and researchers.

## Use Cases

Students convert lecture notes and papers into audio study material. Researchers get audio summaries of paper collections. r/ArtificialIntelligence frequently highlights it as "the most underrated free AI tool."

## The Bottom Line

Completely free and genuinely useful. Upload your documents, get a podcast. No competitors match this specific feature at this price (free). Best for anyone who learns better by listening.
