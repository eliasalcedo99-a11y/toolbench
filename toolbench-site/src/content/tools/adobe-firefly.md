## Overview

Adobe Firefly is the generative AI engine powering creative features across Adobe's suite. Unlike competitors, it's trained exclusively on licensed Adobe Stock content, offering IP indemnity for commercial use — a critical differentiator for professional designers and enterprises.

## Use Cases

Photoshop's Generative Fill is the killer app — extend images, remove objects, and add elements with AI. Designers use it for rapid prototyping, asset creation, and photo retouching. r/photoshop users call Generative Fill "the most useful Photoshop feature in a decade."

## The Bottom Line

Essential for Adobe CC subscribers. The only commercially safe AI image generator with IP indemnity. Standalone quality trails Midjourney, but the Photoshop integration makes it indispensable for professional design workflows.
