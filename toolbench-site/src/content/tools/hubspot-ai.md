HubSpot's bet on Breeze AI is the right one — surface AI inside the CRM context (lead history, deal stage, past emails) instead of bolting it on. The result: outreach personalization that actually uses your data, not generic LLM completions.

The economic story is "if you're already on HubSpot, this is the cheapest AI sales layer you can buy." If you're not, the platform pricing is steep and the lock-in is real. Migration off HubSpot is genuinely difficult — budget for that decision before you commit.
