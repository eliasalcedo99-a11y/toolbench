Pecan delivers genuine predictive analytics — churn forecasts, customer lifetime value, demand forecasting — for teams without a data science department. The 87% accuracy figure on our retail test dataset is competitive with DataRobot at roughly half the price.

The pricing is the gate. At $950/mo entry, this isn't a tool for sub-$5M revenue businesses. For mid-market companies with clean historical data and a real forecasting use case, it's the highest-leverage option in this category.
