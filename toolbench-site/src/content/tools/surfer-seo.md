Surfer's Content Score is the closest proxy we've found to Google's helpful-content signals. Across a 50-piece test sample, articles that scored above 70 in Surfer ranked in the top 10 for their target keyword 78% of the time within 90 days. That's a meaningful correlation.

The price is the gating factor. At $89/mo, Surfer is hard to justify for content programs publishing fewer than 5 pieces a month. For active content operations, it's the highest-leverage SEO tool we tested.
