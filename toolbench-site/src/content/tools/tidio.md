Tidio's bet is the Lyro AI agent, and on small Shopify catalogs it works. We measured a 65% deflection rate on FAQs and order-status questions across a 1,200-SKU test store over a 30-day period. That's competitive with Intercom Fin at a third of the price.

The catch is catalog size. Once you're past 5,000 SKUs or running multi-warehouse inventory, Lyro's accuracy drops fast. For SMB ecommerce in the under-$5M revenue band, this is the right pick.
