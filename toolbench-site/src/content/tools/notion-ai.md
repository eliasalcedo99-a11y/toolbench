## Overview

Notion AI is an AI layer built into Notion's all-in-one workspace. It can write, summarize, answer questions, and automate databases using context from your entire workspace — making it one of the most contextually aware AI assistants.

## Use Cases

Knowledge workers use it for writing assistance, managers summarize team docs, and PMs get AI-powered project updates. r/Notion is highly active with AI feature discussions. Most valuable for heavy Notion users.

## The Bottom Line

Must-have AI add-on for Notion users. $10/mo on top of Notion subscription. The workspace-aware Q&A is the killer feature. Not a standalone AI tool — requires Notion investment.
