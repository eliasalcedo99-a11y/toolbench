## Overview

Figma AI brings generative capabilities into the most popular design tool. Rather than replacing designers, it accelerates repetitive tasks: auto-layout, asset generation, prototyping, and design system maintenance. Integrated directly into the familiar Figma interface.

## Use Cases

Designers use AI for rapid wireframing, auto-layout generation, and asset search. Product teams leverage it for faster prototyping. r/FigmaDesign discusses AI features extensively.

## The Bottom Line

Essential for Figma users. $15/editor/mo Professional unlocks full AI. Not a standalone design AI — it enhances existing Figma workflows. For AI image generation, use Midjourney or DALL-E.
